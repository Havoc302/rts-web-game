Current task: Fix crime being driven by the wrong "unemployment" metric, which masked the tax rate's effect on growth.

- [done] Root cause: CrimeManager used `1 - stats.employmentRate` (jobsFilled / totalJobsProvided) as its unemployment signal. That's a job-vacancy-fill ratio, not resident unemployment — in a city with abundant jobs relative to population it reads as near-zero (e.g. 0.01), which the crime scaler (2.5x) blew up into near-guaranteed crime every tick regardless of tax rate, capping tile crime at 10 and permanently zeroing growthScore via the crime growth penalty. That made it look like tax rate no longer affected growth (both 0% and 100% tax stalled at growthScore 0), while employment rate still visibly moved with tax since jobsMultiplier is computed independently.
- [done] Renamed `CRIME_CONFIG.UNEMPLOYMENT_CRIME_SCALER` to `JOB_SCARCITY_CRIME_SCALER` and changed CrimeManager to drive crime probability off resident job scarcity: `(totalEmployablePopulation - jobsFilled) / totalEmployablePopulation` (residents who want work but can't get any, because there aren't enough jobs), instead of the jobs-side employment rate. A city with more jobs than residents now reads as zero scarcity (low baseline crime only) even before every job slot is filled.
- [done] Updated the one test that toggled this constant to the new name.

Design rule:
- School demand is `round(population * 0.15)`.
- University demand is `round(population * 0.05)`.
- School capacity is staffed School jobs multiplied by `EDUCATION_CONFIG.STUDENT_CAPACITY_PER_JOB` (Libraries are decoupled from student capacity and growth scores, contributing strictly to public happiness).
- University capacity is staffed University jobs multiplied by the same student-seat factor.
- `schoolRatio = min(1, schoolCapacity / schoolDemand)` when demand is nonzero; otherwise zero.
- `uniRatio = min(1, universityCapacity / universityDemand)` when demand is nonzero; otherwise zero.
- Global zone tax multiplier is `1 + schoolRatio * 0.15 + uniRatio * 0.20`, applied to base residential/commercial/industrial/agricultural tax before crime tax loss.
- Partial education coverage scales linearly. Education does not create residents or jobs; it only modifies tax yield.

- [done] Tundra profile: 3-6 small lakes (`lakeRadiusScale: 0.6`), a `mixed` river, and `frozenWater: true`. Forest clusters stay at 0, so there are still no wildfire candidates.
- [done] `Grid.generateLakes` paints lakes as ice when the biome freezes, so tundra lakes are frozen end to end.
- [done] `Grid.freezeRiverBanks` converts only bank tiles (water tiles with at most `ICE_CONFIG.RIVER_BANK_MAX_WATER_NEIGHBORS` open-water neighbours) at `ICE_CONFIG.RIVER_BANK_ICE_CHANCE`, so the flowing centre lane never freezes and downstream pollution keeps moving.
- [done] Ice is not water for placement: `isWaterAdjacent` and `getWaterfrontStatus` ignore it, so a Water Tower must touch open river and a frozen bank forfeits the clean-water bonus. Finding an unfrozen stretch is now a site-selection decision.
- [done] `canPlaceBridge` accepts ice as well as open water, so a frozen reach still has crossings.
- [done] Bare tundra ground renders as brown-grey soil with seeded value-noise frost patches (`ICE_CONFIG.GROUND_PATCH_CHANCE` / `GROUND_PATCH_SCALE`, exported as `tundraGroundIsFrost`). Decorative only: those tiles stay flat and buildable. Arctic ground stays a solid ice sheet.
- [done] Frozen water renders as saturated blue-white with hairline cracks; bridges now draw from the live render loop so ice bridges appear and disappear correctly without invalidating the terrain chunks.
- [done] `getTerrainPercentages` counts ice as water for world-map statistics.
- [done] Tests: `tests/tundra-biome.test.js` rewritten for lakes, bank-only freezing, bridge crossing, water-adjacency and waterfront rules, and the frost-patch palette/determinism. Bump every version source to `0.3.14`.

Notes:
- No unlucky-seed guard was added: a 200x200 tundra map leaves 70-400 legal water-adjacent flat tiles, so sealing every bank is not a practical risk.

Current task: Ocean edge awareness, beaches, bridges, and the Desalination Plant (`0.4.0`).

City maps used to be landlocked: every hex generated a square map with a river and lakes but no sea, so a coastal hex looked identical to an inland one. City maps now inherit their coastline from the hex, and the sea is a real water type that only one building can drink from.

- [done] `OverworldMap.getOceanFacingDirections(id)` classifies the six H3 neighbours by compass bearing into `N, NE, SE, S, SW, NW` and caches the ocean-facing ones. Deterministic per planet seed and cell.
- [done] New `salt_water` and `sand` terrains. `isBuildableTerrain()` makes sand accept roads, zones, and buildings exactly like flat ground.
- [done] `Grid.generateCoast()` paints each ocean-facing edge as a half-plane: salt water 3-10 tiles deep with a wobbling shoreline, then a 1-2 tile beach of sand or rock (`COAST_CONFIG`). Side edges use the matching map side, diagonal edges the matching corner; a hex with all six ocean edges also closes its east and west vertices so a one-hex island is surrounded by sea.
- [done] Rivers and lakes generate before the coast, so a river now ends in a river mouth instead of running into the sea band.
- [done] Salt water is not fresh water: `isWaterAdjacent` rejects it, Water Towers cannot drink it, and `getWaterfrontStatus` still grants the clean-water bonus because people like living beside any water.
- [done] Sewage Plants may discharge into salt water (`allowsSaltWater`), which pollutes the coast and contaminates a Desalination Plant drawing from it.
- [done] Bridges cross fresh water, ice, and salt water. A single bridge run is capped at `COAST_CONFIG.BRIDGE_MAX_TILES` (10) by `Grid.bridgeRunLength`, and the Bridge build panel shows the limit.
- [done] Desalination Plant: $10,000, no tiers, 960 water (two tier-3 pumps), `requiresSaltWaterAdjacent`, upkeep `DESALINATION_OPERATING_COST` = 3x a pump. Allocated as a second `reset: false` water pass after pumps so it only covers the shortfall, and its power draw scales with the water it delivers (0 idle up to `PEAK_POWER` 55).
- [done] `DESALINATION_CONFIG` in config; renderer draws the sea, sand, and the plant; inspector names terrains ("Beach Sand", "Salt Water") and the build panel describes desalination.
- [done] Saves store the coast direction list in the map block, and the base grid used for tile diffs is rebuilt with it, so coastal cities survive save/import.
- [done] Tests: new `tests/coast.test.js` (world edge detection, band geometry, islands, placement, bridges, allocation order, contamination); save test extended with coast round-trips. Bump every version source to `0.4.0`.
